# Code of conduct

Be direct and be kind. Review the work, not the person. Assume the other side has a reason.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions. Maintainers may edit, remove or lock contributions and threads that cross those lines, and may block repeat offenders.

Report problems to support@uniqai.app. Reports are read by the UniqAI team and handled privately.
